## Language Guidelines Applied

1. **Use Simple Words**: Replaced complex terms with everyday language
   - "gamified" → "fun game"
   - "cohorts" → "schools"
   - "metrics" → "numbers and results"
   - "engagement" → "how many students played"

2. **Active Voice**: Made sentences more direct
   - "can be run by" → "can be for"
   - "are available" → "you can do"

3. **Shorter Sentences**: Broke long sentences into smaller chunks

4. **Friendly Tone**: Added enthusiasm and encouragement
   - Added exclamation marks for excitement
   - Used "you" and "your" to be personal
   - Added encouraging phrases like "it's quick and easy!"

5. **Removed Jargon**: Eliminated technical or business terms
   - "XP" → "points"
   - "CSR outcomes" → "help students learn"
   - "aggregated reporting" → "overall numbers"
   - "measurable impact" → "see how students did"

6. **Clear Actions**: Made buttons and links more obvious
   - "Join with a Code" → "I Have a Code"
   - "Login with ease" → "Sign In"

## Testing Recommendations

1. Read all text aloud - it should sound natural and conversational
2. Test with actual children (ages 8-14) to ensure comprehension
3. Check that all instructions are clear and actionable
4. Verify that technical terms are explained when necessary
5. Ensure tone is consistently friendly and encouraging
